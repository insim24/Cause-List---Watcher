ORDER
29.03.2021
Petitioner claims to have been deprived of his valuable property i.e. land
measuring 2-kanals falling under Khasra No. 812/518/328 situated at village
Kokerbagh, Tehsil Khag District Budgam, by forcible occupation by the
respondents for construction of building for Middle School, Kokerbagh and the
petitioner time and again approached the respondents for compensation, which
is denied.
Respondent No. 8/Headmaster Govt. Middle School, Kokerbagh, Tehsil
Khag, District Budgam, has filed reply, stating therein that the petitioner has
donated his land on which the respondents have constructed four buildings and
two latrine blocks but the respondents cannot justify transfer of land in tune with
mandate of law.
Let the respondents justify transfer of land and its occupation in tune with
mandate of law, otherwise the petitioner is entitled for compensation and that too
from the date the land in question has been taken over by the respondents. Let
the needful be bone by or before next date of hearing.
List on 5th of May, 2021.
