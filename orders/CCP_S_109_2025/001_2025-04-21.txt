1. The present contempt petition is instituted by the writ petitioners
who came forward with the institution of writ petition WP(C) No.
463/2025 wherein this court vide an order dated 05.03.2025 while
issuing notice to the writ respondents came forward with a
direction for maintenance of status quo in respect of the position
of the subject matter of the dispute as per the revenue record on
spot. However, in the order dated 05.03.2025, reference to the
description of the land forming subject matter of the writ petition
does not find mention.
2. The petitioners have now come forward with that present
contempt petition filed on 10.04.2025 alleging that the status quo
is being breached by the two contemnors, namely, Syed Manzoor

Ahmed and Syed Mohd Ismail who are the writ respondents No. 3
and 4 in the writ petition. The violation of status quo on the part
of the said contemnors alleged is that they have gathered the
building material at the site of the land which is said to be in
khasra No. 2411/560 of Mozia Azadpore Tehsil & District
Anantnag, purported to be subject matter of writ petition.
3. Issue notice to the respondents 1 and 2 namely Syed Manzoor
Ahmed and Syed Mohd Ismail.
4. Petitioners to furnish registered postal covers within a period of
seven days thereupon the Registrar Judicial, Srinagar to issue
notice to the said two respondents.
5. Tehsildar Anantnag is respondent No. 02 in the writ petition and
as such is also under a direction to ensure status quo at the site.
6. Accordingly, Tehsildar Anantnag is directed to ensure that the
status quo as envisaged by this court in terms of its order dated
05.03.2025 is maintained at the site by both contesting sides i.e.,
writ petitioners and the writ respondents No. 3 and 4.
7. List this contempt petition along with the main writ petition
which is posted on 23.04.2025.
