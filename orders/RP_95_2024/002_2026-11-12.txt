RP 95/2024
1. This is a petition seeking review of the judgment dated 20th
November, 2024 passed in LPAOW No. 92/2018, titled, “Abdul
Hamid Dandroo and Ors vs State of J and K and Ors”, on the
ground that this Court has not considered the matter in its right
perspective.
2. The review is sought, inter alia, on the ground that, consequent
upon dismissal of the appeal, the petitioners have been left with no

efficacious remedy to avail of. It is further contended that, despite
writ petitions involving similar questions of law and fact being
pending adjudication before this Court, the appeal filed by the
petitioners came to be dismissed by virtue of the judgment dated
20th November, 2024, which is the subject matter of the present
review petition.
3. After arguing for a while, the learned counsel appearing for the
petitioners fairly submitted that similar writ petitions have now
been dismissed and, therefore, the ground urged in this regard is not
available.
4. Having heard the learned counsel for the petitioners and perused the
material on record, this Court is of the opinion that no good ground
is made out to recall the judgment dated 20th November, 2024
(supra).
5. This Court has already held that the petitioners have been pursuing
the matter before the Civil Court and having regard to the fact that
issues raised by the petitioners involve determination of
complicated disputed questions of fact, a civil suit is the appropriate
remedy. This Court has thus rightly declined to invoke the
extraordinary jurisdiction vested in it under Article 226 of the
Constitution of India and has, accordingly, dismissed the appeal as
being devoid of any merit.
6. The review petition does not disclose any error apparent on the
record, committed by this Court, while dismissing the appeal vide
judgment dated 20th November, 2024, nor there is any discovery of

new facts which would persuade us to take a view different from
the one already taken for the disposal of the appeal.
7. For all these reasons, we find no merit in this petition and the same
is, accordingly, dismissed.
