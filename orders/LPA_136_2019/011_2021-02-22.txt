The Letters Patent Appeal is reported to be beyond time and the
delay has been sought to be explained by an application filed under
Section 5 of the Limitation Act.
A bear reading of the said application reveals that it has been filed
in a casual manner and averments made therein are vague.
Sh. B. A. Dar, learned counsel for the appellants, at this stage,
submits that he may be permitted to file a supplementary affidavit
explaining the delay to which the otherside may object.
In view of the aforesaid facts and circumstances and in the interest
of justice, we permit Mr. B. A. Dar, learned counsel for the appellants to
file a better affidavit within two weeks to which the objections, if any, be
filed by respondents within a week’s time thereafter.
List LPA No.268/2019 along with this appeal on 2nd April, 2021.
