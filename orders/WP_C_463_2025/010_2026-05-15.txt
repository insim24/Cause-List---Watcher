HON’BLE MR. JUSTICE WASIM SADIQ NARGAL , JUDGE.
ORDER
15.05.2026
CM No. 2682/2026:
01. Mr. J. H. Reshi, learned counsel for the petitioners, fairly submits
that the petitioners have already filed a contempt petition, being
CCP(S) No. 109/2025, which stands clubbed with the instant
application, wherein this Court has already taken cognizance. Vide
order dated 21.04.2025, this Court had directed the Tehsildar,
Anantnag to ensure maintenance of status quo at the site by both
the contesting parties, namely the writ petitioners and respondents
No. 3 and 4, in terms of order dated 05.03.2025 passed by this
Court.
02. It is further submitted by learned counsel for the petitioners that
despite the aforesaid orders dated 05.03.2025 and 21.04.2025, the
respondents are allegedly flouting the same with impunity. Aggrieved
thereof, the petitioners have filed the instant application.

03. Mr. J. H. Reshi, learned counsel for the petitioners, when
confronted with the maintainability of the instant application in light
of the fact that the petitioners have already filed a contempt petition
which stands clubbed with the present application, he fairly submits
that he wants to withdraw the instant application and pursue the
remedy in the contempt petition already filed by the petitioners.
Since filing of the instant application would tantamount to initiating
parallel proceedings and, in view of the fact that this Court has
already taken cognizance in the contempt petition filed by the
petitioners, the instant application is not maintainable and is
accordingly dismissed as withdrawn
04. However, dismissal of the instant application shall not come in the
way of the petitioners in pursuing the contempt petition already filed
by them.
05. Accordingly, the application i.e., CM No. 2682/2026 stands
dismissed as withdrawn.
