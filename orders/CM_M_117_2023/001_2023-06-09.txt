HON’BLE MR. JUSTICE WASIM SADIQ NARGAL, JUDGE.
ORDER
09.06.2023
The learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioners through
the medium of the present petition filed under Article 227, is challenging the
order passed by the Executing Court i.e. Court of Sub-Judge/Chief Judicial
Magistrate, Budgam dated 20th May 2023, which is impugned in the present
petition.
The short submission which has been made by the learned counsel
appearing on behalf of the petitioners is that an ex-parte judgment/decree
came to be passed by the Court of Sub-Judge/Chief Judicial Magistrate,
Budgam, dated 30th October 2017, wherein a direction was issued to the
defendants to give due consideration to the plaintiff under rules in case the
plaintiff falls within one time exception under Rule 5 (b) Sub-Rule (VII) of
Service Regulation 2007 of Jammu and Kashmir Muslim Wakf Board Act.
The Court below while passing the aforesaid order, directed the defendants
to convey the outcome of the said consideration order to the plaintiff within
three months from the date the ex-parte judgment and decree was issued.

The learned counsel appearing on behalf of the petitioners submits
that ex-parte judgment/decree passed by the Court below has since been
complied with and a detailed consideration order was passed and this aspect
of the matter has also been recorded/noted by the Executing Court while
passing the order dated 21st February 2023, wherein it has been observed by
the Chief Judicial Magistrate, Budgam in Para No. 6 that on perusal of the
consideration order dated 21st January 2023 which has been placed on record
by the learned counsel for the judgment debtors, the Court was satisfied that
the judgment cum decree dated 13th October 2017 passed by the Court has
been complied with by the defendants/judgment debtors, however, the
learned counsel for the decree holder was not satisfied with the said
consideration order and prayed that he may be given some time for perusal
of the same.
Once finding has been recorded by the Executing Court that the
judgment cum decree dated 31st October 2017 to the extent of passing of
consideration order has been complied, then what weighed with the same
Executing Court to direct the judgment debtors again for passing fresh
consideration order of the judgment and decree issued vide order dated 13th
October 2017 by observing that the judgment debtors shall ensure the
consideration of the said judgment and decree strictly in terms of Rule 5 (b)
Sub-Rule (VII) of the Service Regulation 2007 of Jammu and Kashmir
Muslim Wakf Board Act, vide order dated 20th May 2023, is not
forthcoming from the record. The observation of the Executing Court by
virtue of the order impugned to issue direction to the judgment debtors to
pass fresh order of consideration is beyond the judgment and decree passed
by the Court below.
Mr. Mohammad Iqbal Dar, the learned counsel appearing on behalf
of the petitioners submits that the order impugned cannot sustain the test of
law and is liable to be set-aside.
Heard the learned counsel for the petitioners at length and perused
the record.
Prima facie case for indulgence is made out.
Notice in the main petition as well as in connected CM, returnable
by or before the next date of hearing. Requisite steps for service within one
week.

List on 28th August 2023 before the Registrar Judicial.
In the meantime, subject to objections from the other side and till
next date of hearing before the Bench, the execution proceedings before the
Court of Sub Judge/Chief Judicial Magistrate, Budgam in File No. 08/E
titled as Manzoor Ahmad Jalali vs Vice Chairman J&K Muslim Waqf Board
& Ors, shall remain stayed.
Modification, Vacation, Alteration on motion.
