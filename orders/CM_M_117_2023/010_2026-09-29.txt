01. Respondent being continuo usly absent, the petition is taken on board
for consideration.
02. Petitioners have invoked supervisory jurisdiction of this Court, under
Article 227 of the Constitution of India,against the execution
proceedings and order dated 20.05.2023 passed by learned Sub-
Judge/Chief Judicial Magistrate, Budgam [“the trial Court”] in file
No. 08/E (Application for Execution of Decree) titled “Manzoor
Ahmad Jalali vs. Vice Chairman, J&K Muslim Waqf Board and
Ors.”.
03. Learned counsel for the petitioners has fairly admitted that learned
trial Court in terms of judgment and decree dated 30.10.2017 directed
the petitioners to consider the claim of the respondent, plaintiff in the
suit, provided it falls within one time exception in terms of Rule 5 (b)
Sub Rule (VII) of Service Regulation 2007 of Jammu and Kashmir
Waqf Board.
04. It appears from the consideration order dated 21.01.2023, passed by
the petitioners that case of the respondent/plaintiff came to be
considered in the light of legal position and the relevant rules and
regulations and he was not found eligible for any kind of pensionary
benefits. The consideration order, however, nowhere reflects that

2 CM(M) No. 117/2023
claim of the respondent has been considered strictly in terms of the
judgment passed by the trial Court that whether he falls within one
time exception under Rule 5 (b) Sub Rule (VII) of Service
Regulations 2007 of Jammu and Kashmir Waqf Board, 2007. It is
precisely for this reason that learned trial Court in terms of the
impugned order has directed the petitioners to reconsider the claim of
the respondent strictly in terms of the judgment and decree passed by
the trial Court.
05. Having regard to the aforesaid, I do not find any illegality or
impropriety in the impugned order. Hence, present petition is
dismissed and impugned order is upheld.
06. Disposed of.
