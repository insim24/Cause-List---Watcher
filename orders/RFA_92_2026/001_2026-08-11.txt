ORDER

1. Learned counsel for the respondents has raised a preliminary
objections with regard to the maintainability of the present
miscellaneous appeal on the ground that the Supreme Court
in case titled Mangluram Dewangan Vs. Surendra Singh and
Others reported in (2011) 2 SCC 773 and this Court in the case
titled Maya Ram Vs. Nanak Chand and Others reported in 1992
SCC Online J&K 15 have held that when a suit is dismissed as
having abated on account of death of one of several plaintiffs,
on the ground that right to sue does not survive upon
remaining plaintiffs, it amounts to adjudication of rights of
the parties and as such, the order passed falls within the

definition of decree as provided in section 2(2) of the CPC,
which is appealable in terms of Order XLI of CPC.
2. Confronted with the aforesaid legal position, learned counsel
for the appellant has submitted that the instant appeal may be
treated as a Regular Appeal in terms of Section XLI of CPC.
The prayer is allowed and the present appeal is directed to be

assign the nomenclature of RFA to the present appeal
