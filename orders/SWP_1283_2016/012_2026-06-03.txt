ORDER

Due to paucity of time, the matter could not be considered today.
Learned counsel appearing for the private respondent has submitted that on
account of pendency of this Writ petition, the respondents are not taking a decision
with regard to the promotion/Regularization against the post of editor
Urdu/Persian.
A perusal of the interim order dated 22.06.2016 would reveal that the
official respondents have not been prohibited from making promotion or
regularization against the post of Editor Urdu/Persian. In fact, the Court has
directed that in case any promotion/regularization is envisaged against the post of
Editor Urdu/Persian, the same shall be made strictly in accordance with the rules

2020:JKLHC-SGR:972
in vogue and further it has been directed that the case of the petitioner shall also
be considered. The said Order passed by this Court is explicit inasmuch as the
official respondents have been given liberty to make promotions/regularizations
strictly in accordance with the rules.
Be listed 20th of July, 2026.
