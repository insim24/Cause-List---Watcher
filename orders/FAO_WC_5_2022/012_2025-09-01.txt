01. The respondent No. 4-Deputy Labour
Commissioner, Kashmir who is an ex-officio
Commissioner under Employees Compensation Act,
1923, was directed to send the record of the case

Deputy Labour Commissioner, Kashmir.

Advocate General is requested by this Court to take
notice of the default on part of the Deputy Labour
Commissioner, Kashmir in the matter of sending the

justice, to be taken serious cognizance in case by the
next date of hearing the requisite record does not
come forth from the end of the Deputy Labour
Commissioner, Kashmir.
04. A copy of this order be given by the learned
Registrar Judicial, Srinagar, to Alla Ud Din Ganai,
learned Additional Advocate General for notice and
compliance at the end of the Deputy Labour
Commissioner, Kashmir.
