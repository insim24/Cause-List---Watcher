(ORDER)
18.08.2026
01. The petitioners are seeking indulgence of the Court for allotment of
General Provident Fund numbers in their favour and also seeking direction to
release their salary after deduction of 8% of basic pay towards General
Provident Fund.
02. Vide order dated 10 March 2025, by way of an interim arrangement, the
respondents were directed to initiate and process the respective cases of the
petitioners for allotment of General Provident Fund numbers and also to
release their salary.
03. In compliance with the aforesaid order, the respondents have produced a
communication dated 25 May 2026, whereby they have appointed a nodal
officer to collect the requisite information for allotting the General Provident
Fund numbers and to make necessary deductions and release the salary in their
favour. In this regard, respondents only seek time to do the needful.

04. Keeping in view the controversy involved in the case, this writ petition
is taken on board and disposed of with a direction to the respondents to allot
the General Provident Fund numbers and also release the salary under norms
within a period of four weeks from the date a copy of this order is made
available.
05. The writ petition is disposed of, on the above terms.
06. Interim direction(s), if any, shall stand vacated.
07. Consequently, nothing survives for further adjudication in the contempt
petition. Accordingly, the contempt petition is also closed.
