1. After arguing the matter for some time, learned counsel for the petitioner
seeks permission to withdraw the present petition, with liberty to file a fresh
petition, as M/s Al-Huda Electrical and Maintenance, whose technical
qualification in the NIT in question is under challenge, has not been
impleaded as a party respondent. The petitioner has sought quashing of the
decision of the respondents declaring the said firm technically qualified,
along with all consequential actions, including the opening and consideration
of its financial bid and its placement as L-1.
2. The non-impleadment of M/s Al-Huda Electrical and Maintenance, in the
facts and circumstances of the case, constitutes a serious flaw in the
maintainability of the present petition. When confronted with this aspect,
learned counsel for the petitioner seeks withdrawal of the petition, with
liberty to file a fresh petition by impleading the necessary party.
3. In view of the submission made by learned counsel for the petitioner, the
present petition is dismissed as withdrawn, with liberty to file a fresh
petition by impleading the necessary party.
