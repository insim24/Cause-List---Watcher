From the reading of order dated 28th May, 2019, passed by this Court, it
transpires that prior to the filing of the instant petition under Section 561-A
Cr.PC, the petitioner had filed the petition bearing 561-A No.213/2017, which
was decided by this Court on 17th December, 2017. It is in this context that this
Court called upon the petitioner to address the Court on the issue of
maintainability of second petition challenging the same FIR.
With a view to appreciate the issue formulated by this Court, it is
necessary to peruse the record of 561-A No.213/2017, decided on 17th
December, 2017.
Let the Registry tag the record of aforesaid petition along-with this
petition for its consideration on the next date of hearing.
List on 27.08.2021.
In the meanwhile, Ms. Padroo, learned AAG, shall file response.
