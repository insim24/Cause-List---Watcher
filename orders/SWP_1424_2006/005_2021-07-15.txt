CM No. 2068/2021
In the instant application, applicant is seeking recalling of
order passed on 29.06.2020, whereby the writ petition SWP No.
1424/2006 has been transferred to CAT Bench Jammu. The
application is made on the ground that the instant matter pertains to
ReT Scheme and does not fall within the jurisdiction of the Central
Administrative Tribunal, Jammu, Bench.
In view of above the order dated 29.06.2020, whereby the writ
petition has been transferred to CAT Bench Jammu, is recalled and
writ petition SWP No. 1424/2006 is restored to its original position.

Registry to send a copy of this order to Registrar, CAT Bench,
Jammu for transmitting the records of the writ petition back to this
Court, for disposal, if already transmitted.
CM disposed of.
List SWP No. 1424/2006 on 28.10.2021.
