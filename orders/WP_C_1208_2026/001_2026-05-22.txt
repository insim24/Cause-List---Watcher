01. This is an application filed by the applicants/ petitioners
seeking permission of this Court to file the present writ
petition in representative capacity.
02. For the reasons stated in the application coupled with
submissions made at Bar, same is allowed and the
applicants/ petitioners are permitted to file the writ petition
in representative capacity.

03. Notice to be issued through publication in two Daily Local
newspapers having wide circulation in the area at the
expenses to be borne by the applicants/petitioners.
04. CM is, accordingly, disposed of.
WP(C) No. 1208/2026
05. Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and perused the
material on record.
06. Issue notice, which is accepted by Mr. T.M.Shamsi, learned
DSGI, on behalf of the respondents.
07. Before proceeding further in the matter, this Court deems it
appropriate to direct Mr. T.M.Shamsi, learned DSGI,
appearing for the respondents to file status report at the first
instance within two weeks with respect to the averments
made in the instant writ petition.
08. In addition, it has been brought to the notice of this Court by
learned counsel fo r the petitioners that petitioners have filed
various representations from time to time and the latest
representation on 13th January, 2026, which, till date have
not been accorded due consideration.
09. Learned counsel for the petitioners fairly submits that he will
feel satisfied in case a direction is issued to the respondents
7 to 9 to accord due consideration on the latest
representation dated 13th January, 2026, which is alleged to
have been filed by the petitioners before them.

10. Let the aforesaid representation be accorded due
consideration within notice period and the consideration
order to be passed on the said representation shall also be
placed on record along with reply.
11. List on 10th July, 2026.
