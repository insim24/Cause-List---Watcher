1. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner in respect of the
matter.
2. The case of the petitioner, in nutshell, is that he and his brother
are reputed businessmen who are running multiple ventures across
the globe, including the FIL Industries, having its substantial
business in the Union Territory of Jammu and Kashmir. That he,
through his firm, i.e., FIL Industries Private Limited, has availed
various loan facilities from Large Credit Unit of Jammu & Kashmir
Bank Private Limited for smooth operation of his ventures, in
connection whereof multiple mortgage deeds have been executed by
him in favour of the Jammu & Kashmir Bank Private Limited as
collateral security. That in the year 2022, he applied for enhancement
of cash credit limit from Rs. 70.22 crores to rupees 76.22 Crores to
upgrade and modernize its existing food and beverage division at
SIDCO electronics complex at Rangreth, and also to enhance its

working capital facility. That a mortgage deed as collateral security
dated 28-07-2022 was executed and registered with Sub-Registrar
Sopore in favour of the Jammu & Kashmir Bank Private Limited on
06-10-2022. That stamp duty and registration fees for the aforesaid
mortgage deed dated 28-07-2022 was exempted in pursuance of the
Government Notification No. SO241 dated 16th of July, 2021,
whereby the Government directed that there shall be remission of
duty chargeable under the Stamps Act, 1977, for new as well as
existing industrial units undertaking substantial expansion in case of
documents/ instruments related to land transactions in Government
Industrial Estates, including lease/ mortgage deeds executed by the
said industrial units under Jammu & Kashmir Industrial Policy 2021-
30, notified by the Government vide Order No. 117-IND of 2021,
dated 19th April, 2021.
That he and his brother again, in the year 2022, applied for
grant of ad hoc loan facility, whereupon the Jammu & Kashmir Bank
Private Limited, sanctioned the said facility to the tune of rupees 10
Crore over and above the already sanctioned limit, in connection
whereof the mortgage deed dated 28-10-2022 was executed and
registered before the Sub-Registrar Sopore on 14-11-2022. That the
stamp duty and registration fees on the said mortgage deed was again
exempted by the said office of the Sub-Registrar concerned in
pursuance to the aforesaid Government Notification dated 16th of
July, 2021, forming Annexure- I to the petition.
That on 12-05-2023, Government issued a clarification with
respect to the Government Order No. 117-IND of 2021, dated 19th of

April, 2021, pertaining to exemption of stamp duty and registration
fees, of industrial units, within the jurisdiction of industrial estate
whose mortgaged property falls outside the Government Industrial
Estate, and directed that where the industrial units falling within the
industrial estate but the proposed mortgage property falls outside the
industrial estate would not be exempted from the payment of stamp
duty and registration fees. That the copy of the said clarification
order issued by the Government has been annexed to the petition as
Annexure- III.
That again, in the year 2023, the Jammu & Kashmir Bank
sanctioned a short-term loan of rupees 8 Crores in his favour against
the mortgage deed that came to be executed on 19-07-2023 and
registered before the learned Sub-Registrar Sopore on 31-07-
2023.That he, as per the clarification order/ communication dated 12-
05-2023 dated 19th of April, 2021 issued by the Government
regarding payment of stamp duty and registration fees, accordingly
paid rupees 4 lacs as stamp duty and 9.60 lacs as registration fees.
That the copy of the said mortgage deed dated 19-07-2023 is annexed
with his petition and marked as Annexure IV.
That he, time and again, approached the respondents seeking
clarification as regards the liability of stamp duty and registration
fees in respect of the mortgage deeds executed by him and his brother
in the year 2022, when the stamp duty and registration fee were
exempted by the office of the learned Sub-Registrar in pursuance of
the then existing Government Notification dated 16th July, 2021. That
he, however, paid the stamp duty as required by the office of the Sub-

Registrar concerned on the mortgage deed executed and registered
subsequently to the clarification order/ communication dated 12-05-
2023. That he has voluntary under a bona fide intention, approached
the Office of the concerned Sub-Registrar to seek clarification as to
whether he needs to pay the registration fee and stamp duty on the
earlier mortgage deeds or not. That ambiguity regarding the order of
exemption and the subsequent revocation of the same still persists, as
the earlier order of exemption had been issued by the learned
Financial Commissioner to the Government, Department of Finance,

he has already filed a representation with the respondents seeking

which no stamp duty and registration fee was chargeable, and
expressed his intention that he is ready to pay the stamp duty and
registration fee also on such earlier mortgage deeds, if at all required
under law.
3. The petitioner has accordingly prayed for the issuance of
writs/ orders in the nature of mandamus:-
1) for commanding the respondents to decide his application/
representation seeking clarification as to whether the order
dated 12-05-2023, issued by respondent No. 3, revoking the
exemption of stamp duty and registration fee in respect of
the mortgaged property situated outside the Industrial Units,
would operate retrospectively and would cover the

mortgage deeds executed by him and his brother in the year
2022 and registered before the learned Sub-Registrar,
Sopore.
2) for directing the respondents to require and receive from
him the proper stamp and registration fee on such earlier
mortgage deeds, if required in accordance with law.
4. The learned counsel for the petitioner, during his preliminary
arguments, submitted that the petitioner and his brother are
awakened, well-reputed, highly respected and law-abiding citizens,
who have volunteered to approach the respondents seeking the
clarification as regards the payment of stamp duty and registration
fee in respect of the mortgage deeds executed by them in favour of
Jammu & Kashmir Bank Private Limited in consideration of loan
facilities, prior to the passing of the clarification order dated 12-05-
2023, and have shown their willingness to pay the requisite stamp
duty and registration fee, if so required to be paid in respect of the
earlier mortgage deeds, on which the said duties were exempted by
the office of the concerned Sub-Registrar in pursuance of
Government Order No. 117-IND of 2021 dated 19th of April, 2021.
5. The learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that the instant
petition, in view of its facts and the relief sought herein, can be
disposed of even at this threshold stage by passing of appropriate
directions, which are likely to be in the interest of the respondents,
and the action under which directions was already needed to be taken
under rules by the respondents out of their statutory obligations,
especially upon being represented by the petitioner.

6. In the facts and circumstances of the case, the Court is of the
opinion that, as rightly suggested by the learned counsel for the
petitioner, the matter can be disposed of even at this threshold stage
by passing of appropriate directions, which will be advantageous to
the respondents/ UT of J&K.
7. For the foregoing discussion, the instant petition is disposed
of with a direction to the respondents, especially respondent Nos. 3
and 4, i.e., Inspector General of Registration, Jammu and Kashmir
and Sub-Registrar, Sopore, District Baramulla, to address the
application/representation of the petitioner, already filed or to be filed
by him, seeking clarification regarding his liability to pay the stamp
duty and registration fee on the mortgage deeds executed by him and
his brother and registered by the learned Sub-Registrar, Sopore,
Baramulla, prior to the clarification Order/Communication dated 12-
05- 2023, revoking the exemption already availed by them on such
earlier mortgage deeds in pursuance of Government Order No. 117-
IND of 2021 (SO241) dated 19th of April, 2021, and to require and
receive the proper stamp duty/ registration fee from them, if at all
required under law. The respondents Nos. 3 and 4 are directed to take
action in the matter within a period of eight weeks
8. Disposed of.
