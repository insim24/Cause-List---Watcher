CM Nos. 1768/2024 and CM No. 1769/2024.
01. Through the medium of the present order, two applications one
seeking condonation of delay in filing the restoration application, and the other
seeking restoration of the main Writ petition bearing IA No. 2 of 2016 are
proposed to be disposed of together.
02. In the application, it has been submitted by the petitioner that IA
No. 3 of 2016, seeking recall of the order dated 7th May 2016 passed by this Court
in SWP No. 2224 of 2014, was not listed before this Court after the year 2016. It
has been submitted that the being a service matter, the petitioner was under the
impression that the said application had been transferred to the Central
Administrative Tribunal, Srinagar, and accordingly, he was attempting to trace the

2016:JKLHC-SGR:14
file before the Tribunal. It was only in the second week of March 2024, that the
petitioner came to know that IA No. 3 of 2016 had already been dismissed for
non-prosecution by this Court vide order dated 7th of February 2023. On this
ground, the delay in filing the application for restoration of IA No. 3 of 2016, has
been sought.
03. In the objections filed by the respondent, it has been submitted that
the cause shown by the applicant is not genuine. It has been submitted that, since
the petitioner had filed the application before this Court, there was no reason for
him to search the file from the Central Administrative Tribunal, Srinagar. It has
been further submitted that the petitioner has failed to annex any material or proof
in support of his assertions.
04. A perusal of the record shows that the Writ petition filed by the
petitioner was disposed of vide order dated 7th May 2016. It also appears that the
subject matter of the Writ petition pertains to a service dispute, which is amenable
to the jurisdiction of the Central Administrative Tribunal. The record also reveals
that the petitioner had moved an application seeking recall of the order dated 7th
May 2016, which came to be registered as IA No. 2 of 2016. The said application
was listed before this Court on 28th June 2016, and the notice was issued to the
other side. Thereafter, it was listed on 4th of July 2016, on which date status-quo
in respect of the post held by the private respondent was directed to be maintained.
It appears that the petitioner’s application was never listed thereafter until 7th
February 2023, on which date it came to be dismissed for non-prosecution.
05. It is a fact of common knowledge that, after passing of the
Reorganisation Act, 2019, the Central Administrative Tribunal was established at

2016:JKLHC-SGR:14
Srinagar, and all service matters pending before this Court came to be transferred
to the said Tribunal. Therefore, the submission of the petitioner that he was under
the impression that his case had also been transferred to the Tribunal cannot be
said to be unfounded. Since a large number of service matters were transferred by
this Court to the Central Administrative Tribunal, Srinagar, the submission of the
petitioner that he was attempting to trace his file before the Tribunal appears to be
reasonable and believable. The petitioner’s application came to be listed before
this Court after a lapse of seven years. Normally, notice should have been issued
to the petitioner before dismissing it for non-prosecution.
06. In the aforesaid circumstances, the cause shown by the petitioner for
not filing the application for restoration of IA No. 3 of 2016 within the prescribed
period of limitation appears to be plausible
07. Accordingly, both applications are allowed. The delay in filing the
restoration application is condoned, and IA No. 3 of 2016 is restored, subject to
the petitioner depositing a sum of Rs. 1,500/– in the Advocates’ Welfare Fund
within a period of ten days from today.
08. Both the applications are disposed of.
09. IA No. 3 of 2016 shall be listed for consideration before this Court on
2nd of December 2025.
