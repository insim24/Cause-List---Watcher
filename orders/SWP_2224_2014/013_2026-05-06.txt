CM No. 03/2026:
By virtue of the instant application, the writ petitioner seeks revival of the
main writ petition, which came to be dismissed vide order dated 07.05.2016 on
the basis of the statement made by learned counsel for the private respondents
that the petitioner had been appointed as Assistant Professor in the Higher
Education Department.
It is the case of the applicant/writ petitioner that although he was
appointed as Assistant Professor in the Higher Education Department, he did not
join the said post and he continues to work in the respondent-department.

In view of the assertions made by the applicant/writ petitioner, the order
dated 07.05.2016 passed in the main writ petition, whereby the writ petition was
disposed of on the premise that the writ petitioner had joined the post of
Assistant Professor in the Higher Education Department, deserves to be recalled
and is accordingly recalled. Consequently, the writ petition is restored to its
original number.
The CM is, accordingly, disposed of.
List the main writ petition for consideration on 01.06.2026.
