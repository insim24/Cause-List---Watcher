CM 2585/2024
Instant CM has been moved to this writ petition by Mr. Syed Manzoor
Ahmed, advocate seeking impleadment of Ms. Masarat Jahan, proprietor
Jehlum Valley Filling Station as party respondent no. 08 to the instant writ.
It appears that the instant CM has not been listed till date before the
Hon’ble Court for hearing/consideration.
WP(C) 371/2024
Perusal of the file reveals that steps for effecting service upon the
respondents 04 and 05 in terms of the Hon’ble Court order dated 26.02.2024
and order dated 22.03.2024 have not been taken by the counsel for the
petitioner till date.
However, today the learned counsel for the petitioner submits that he
wants to delete the respondents 04 and 05 from the array of the respondents,
as such; he does not want to affect the service upon the aforementioned
respondents.
As such, CM 2585/2024 be listed in the first instance before the
Hon’ble Court along with the main petition on 22.05.2024.
Meanwhile, reply if filed by the respondents 01 to 03, 06 and 07, same
be taken on the record.
