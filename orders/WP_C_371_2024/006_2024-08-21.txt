1. Instant application filed by applicant-Masarat Jahan is for her
impleadment as party respondent in petition, WP(C) No. 371/2024.
2. Applicant prays for impleadment on the ground that a new petroleum
outlet commissioned by Indian Oil Corporation allotted in favour of
non-applicant/petitioner is adjacent to her petroleum outlet, which
would affect her business and livelihood. The non-applicant/petitioner’s
petroleum outlet is stated to have been commissioned in violation of
stipulated guidelines issued by Ministry of Road Transport and
Highways and also without getting due permission from concerned
departments, particularly Ministry of Defence and Ministry of Road,
Transport and Highways.
3. It is also averred in the application that applicant has filed a writ
petition (WP(C) No. 2925/2023, wherein she has sought cancellation of
outlet permission granted to non-applicant/petitioner, which is pending
adjudication before this Court.
4. Applicant is aggrieved of communication whereby permission has been
granted to non-applicant/petitioner to run his petroleum outlet
commissioned by Indian Oil Corporation. The applicant cannot be
impleaded as party respondent on the aforementioned grounds as she

has already filed an independent writ petition seeking cancellation of
non-applicants/petitioner’s petroleum outlet, therefore, in my opinion,
applicant is not a necessary party to be impleaded in the present
proceedings.
5. In the above backdrop, no ground is made out in this application for
impleadment of applicant as party respondent in petition, WP(C) No.
371/2024 and the same is, accordingly, dismissed.
