CM No. 7684/2019
The instant application has been filed, seeking condonation of delay of
366 days in filing LPA No. 329/2019.
CM No. 3800/2019
The instant application has been filed, seeking condonation of delay of
209 days in filing LPA No. 136/2019.
CM No. 6763/2019
The instant application has been filed, seeking condonation of delay of
366 days in filing LPA No. 268/2019.
Since it is admitted by learned counsel for the parties that all the
aforestated LPAs have been filed against a common judgment dated 31-
08-2018 passed by the learned Single Judge in OWP No. 386/2014, OWP
No. 479/2011, OWP No. 623/2011 and OWP No. 1725/2012 which is
subject matter of a separate LPA bearing LPA No. 211/2019, in which

also an application seeking condonation of delay bearing CM No.
5270/2019 has been filed, which has been allowed vide order dated 16-
12-2019.
Objections have been filed in CM No. 7684/2019 and CM No.
3800/2019 by the non applicants resisting these applications seeking
condonation of delay. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the non-
applicants that the applications are vague in nature and prayed to be
rejected. However, in view of the application seeking condonation of
delay bearing CM No. 5270/2019 in a clubbed matter having been
already allowed, therefore, proprietary demands that applications seeking
condonation of delay in all the connected LPAs have to be allowed and
delay is to be condoned.
The delay in all the matters being huge, therefore, if these applications
are to be allowed, costs need to be imposed. Subject to depositing of cost
of Rs. 5000/- for each of the applications by the appellants in the account
of J&K Yateem Khaanah bearing No. 0372040100020529, maintained
with J&K Bank Branch Bemina, Srinagar, within a period of four weeks
from today, CM No. 7684/2019, CM No. 3800/2019 and CM No.
6763/2019 are allowed and delay is condoned.
The LPA No. 329/2019, LPA No. 136/2019 and LPA No. 268/2019
have already been diarized.
