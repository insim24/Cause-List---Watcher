HON’BLE MR. JUSTICE RAHUL BHARTI, JUDGE.
ORDER
13.10.2025
01. In order to figure out the status of khasra No. 323,
mauza Shalimar Bagh, this Court is constrained to direct
the personal appearance of Tehsildar concerned along
with the concerned Patwari to come along with the
revenue record of khasra No. 323 of Estate/Mauza
Shalimar.

02. This direction is being issued in contrast to the
observation of the Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir in
his order No. Divcom/RA-AVS(10)2014 dated
16.05.2014 wherein it was noted that, as per the report
submitted by the Additional Deputy Commissioner
Srinagar, khasra No. 323 is situated in Estate Nishat
Bagh. Whether Shalimar Bagh comes under
Mauza/Estate Nishat Bagh or if revenue Estate Mauza
Shalimar Bagh is distinct is for the Tehsildar concerned
to apprise to this Court.
03. In addition, this Court directs Mr. Alla-ud-din Ganai,
learned Additional Advocate General to submit the file of
enquiry report No. TCS/LAS/1797-N/34 dated
05.05.2014 of the Additional Deputy Commissioner,
Srinagar which was submitted to the Divisional
Commissioner, Kashmir leading to the passing of an order
No. Divcom/RA-AVS(10)2014 dated 16.05.2014.

04. Requisite compliance be done by next date of hearing.
05. The physical record of OWP No. 1369/2014 has been
attached to the present file.
06. However, the record of OWP No. 864/2012 also needs
to be attached to the present file. The Registry is directed
to get the said file attached as well.
07. List on 10th of November, 2025.
(Rahul Bharti)
