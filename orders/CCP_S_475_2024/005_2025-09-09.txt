ORDER
09.09.2025
01. Four brothers, namely, Ali Mohammad
Malik, Ghulam Rasool Malik, Ghulam Hassan
Malik, and Ghulam Ahmad Malik all being sons of
Mohammad Malik had come forward with a writ
petition OWP No.864/2012 before this Court which
had resulted in its disposal vide an order dated
12.02.2014 with a direction to the writ respondent
Nos. 2 and 3 therein i.e. the Divisional
Commissioner, Kashmir and the Deputy
Commissioner, Srinagar to verify the title of the

land belonging to said petitioners measuring 01
kanal 91/2 marlas under Survey No. 323 min
situated at Shalimar Bagh, Srinagar alleged to have
been taken over by the writ respondent No. 6-
Director Floriculture Department, Srinagar and writ
respondent No. 7-Superintendent Gardens,
Floriculture Department, Srinagar and in case after
verification of the land in question same was to be
found to be the proprietary land of the petitioners,
then to pay the compensation upon assessment by
the Collector Land Acquisition, Srinagar at market
value.
02. It further came to be directed in terms of
said order dated 12.02.2014 that compensation if
payable to the petitioners for the said land then the
same to be recovered from the above said writ
respondent Nos. 6 and 7.
03. Purportedly acting in compliance to the
aforesaid writ court order dated 12.02.2014 passed
in OWP No. 864/2012, the writ respondent No. 2-
Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir, had come
forward with an order No. DivCom/RA-AVS

/(10)/2014 dated 16.05.2014, operative part of
which is reproduced hereunder:
“Whereas; the report of the
Additional Deputy Commissioner,
Srinagar, records produced by Tehsildar
(North) Srinagar and the statement of
petitioner were thoroughly perused and
examined. The claim of the petitioners for
payment of compensation has not been
found tenable and is accordingly
rejected.”
04. This outcome in the form of order dated
16.05.2014 passed by the writ respondent No. 2-
Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir, resulted in
institution of 2nd writ petition OWP No. 1369/2014
by two of the original four writ petitioners of OWP
No. 864/2012 joined by the legal heirs of rest of the
two writ petitioners total numbering 16 thereby
challenging the impugned order No. DivCom/RA-
AVS /(10)/2014 dated 16.05.2014 of the
Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir, and further
asking for compensation for said land measuring 1
kanal and 91/2 marlas.

05. Same set of the respondents as figuring in
OWP No. 864/2012, came to be arrayed as
respondents in the 2nd writ petition OWP No.
1369/2014.
06. Said writ petition OWP No. 1369/2014
came to be disposed of on merits by the writ court
in terms of judgment dated 25.07.2024 with
operative portion reading as under:
“11. Viewed thus, for what has been
observed, considered and analyzed
hereinabove, the petition in hand deserves to
be allowed. Accordingly, the petition is allowed
as a consequence whereof, the impugned order
No. Divcom/RA-AVS(10)2014 dated
16.05.2014 is quashed and the respondents
are commanded to process and settle the case
of the petitioners for payment of compensation
qua the land in question measuring 01 kanal
and 9 ½ marlas, covered under Survey No.323
Min, strictly in tune with the directions passed
by this Court in OWP No.864/2012 in terms of
judgment dated 12.02.2014. The said exercise
be commenced and concluded preferably
within a period of three months from the date
of a copy of this order is produced by the
petitioners before the respondents.
12. Disposed of.”

07. It is by reference to this finality attained
judgment dated 25.07.2024 that the compliance
from the writ respondents’ end failed to come up
resulting in institution of the present contempt
petition.
08. Notice in the present contempt petition came
to be issued in terms of an order dated 06.12.2024
and the service came to be completed as per
Registrar Judicial, Srinagar’s order dated
03.03.2025.
09. On behalf of contemnors/respondents Mr.
Faheem Nisar Shah, learned Government Advocate
came to cause appearance on 25.03.2025 and time
was extended for the respondents/contemnors to
file statement of facts/compliance report.
10. Today, it is Mr. Furqan Yaqub Sofi, learned
Government Advocate who is now representing the
respondents/contemnors.
11. Mr. Furqan Yaqub Sofi, learned Government
Advocate is directed to come forward with
Statement of Facts/Compliance Report whatsoever
positively failing which this court shall be
constrained to pass an order directing personal

appearance of the defaulting respondents/
contemnors for non-filing of the Statement of
Facts/Compliance Report.
12. List again on 26.09.2025.
13. A copy of this order be forwarded by the
learned Registrar Judicial, Srinagar to the
respondent/contemnor No. 2-Divisional
Commissioner, Kashmir, the respondent/
contemnor No. 4-Director Floriculture Department,
Srinagar and also to Mr. Furqan Yaqub Sofi,
learned Government Advocate for the sake of notice
and needful compliance.
