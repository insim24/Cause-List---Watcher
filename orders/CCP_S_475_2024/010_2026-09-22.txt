1. This contempt petition came to be filed before this Court way back
in December, 2014 for proceeding against the respondents/alleged
contemnors for their alleged willful defiance and the breach of the
judgment of this Court dated 25.07.2024 passed in OWP No.
1369/2014. The judgment, for the alleged defiance and breach of
which, the instant contempt petition stands filed so as to proceed
against the alleged contemnors in accordance with the law, directs
the respondents in the basic petition to process and settle the case of
the petitioners for payment of compensation qua the land in
question measuring 1 Kanal and 9 and a Half Marla covered under
Survey No. 323 Min situated at Shalimar Bagh strictly in tune with
the directions passed by this Court on 12.02.2014 in OWP No.
864/2012. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the petitioners
that vide order dated 12.02.2014 passed on OWP No. 864/2012,

while disposing of the same, the respondents were directed to verify
the title of the petitioners vis-à-vis the subject land and, subject to
the verification, pay the reasonable compensation to be decided by
the Collector at the prevalent market rate. It is submitted that while
misinterpreting the said judgment to the prejudice and injustice of
the petitioners, the respondents proceeded to pass the order No.
Divcom/RA-AVS (10) 2014 dated: 16.05.2014 denying their lawful
claim, which prompted the petitioners to approach again before this
Court with the subsequent petition OWP 1369/2014. That finally
while disposing of the aforesaid petition, the order dated 16.05.2014
was quashed with the direction to the respondents to process and
settle the case of the petitioners for payment of compensation vis-à-
vis the subject land directly in time with the directions passed earlier
by this Court in OWP No. 864/2012 in terms of the judgment dated
12.02.2014. The respondents were directed to complete the exercise
preferably within a period of three months from the date a copy of
the judgment dated 25.07.2024 is produced before them.
2. It is submitted by the learned counsel that the judgment dated
25.07.2024 passed with clarification and unequivocal direction to the
respondents is still awaiting its compliance at the hands of the
respondents.
3. Tehsildar (North) Srinagar who was the respondent No. 4 in the
main petition, is present along with the record and has submitted in
the open court, on the basis of the perusal of the record, that
petitioners are the owners of the subject land which is recorded as

bedzar and has already been taken over by the Government in
connection with the development of the park. It is submitted by the
learned counsel for the petitioners that the park developed on the
land of the petitioners was earlier looked after by the Floriculture
Department but now falls within the domain of Srinagar Municipal
Corporation
4. The impleadment of the present incumbent in the Office of the
Commissioner Srinagar Municipal Corporation Faz Lul Haseeb
(IAS) appears to be imperative for the just disposal of the instant
contempt petition.
5. The former Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir, stands already
arrayed as contemnor in the instant petition.
6. The present Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir Mr. Anshul Garg
(IAS) is also impleaded as party respondent in the contempt petition.
7. As per the judgment in question, the respondents therein were
directed to pay the compensation to the petitioners to be assessed by
the Collector concerned at the market rate.
8. Now in the facts and circumstances of the case, the newly impleaded
persons namely Anchul Garg, Divisional Commissioner, Kashmir
and Faz Lul Haseeb, Commissioner, Srinagar Municipal
Coroportion have to account for the implementation of the judgment
in question
9. Both these persons stand impleaded as respondents in the contempt
petition.

10. Registry is directed to issue notices to both the respondents in the
contempt petition supported with the copies of the petition and the
judgment dated 25.07.2024, for filing the compliance
report/statement of facts by the next date of hearing.
11. List on 28th October, 2026.
12. Tehsildar (North) Srinagar is relieved and his personal appearance
for the time being is dispensed with.
