ORDER
23.05.2022
Petitioner has challenged the order dated 21.04.2022, passed by the Special
Mobile Magistrate, Pulwama, whereby learned Magistrate has granted
maintenance under section 488 CrPC to the respondents. It has been contended
by the learned counsel for the petitioner that as per the provisions of 488 CrPC
no maintenance can be awarded in favour of a major son as such the impugned
order of the trial Magistrate is not in accordance with the law. Learned counsel
for the petitioner has submitted that respondent No.3 is admittedly major son of
the petitioner and respondent No.1 so he is not entitled to the maintenance.
There appears to be some merit in the contentions raised by the learned
counsel for the petitioner.
Issue notice to the respondent. Mr. Gash Asrar ul Haq, learned advocate
who is on caveat accepts the notice. The caveat is discharged. He seeks and is
granted four weeks’ time to file the response. In the meanwhile impugned order
of the learned Magistrate to the extent of awarding maintenance in favour of the

respondent No.3 shall remained stayed. However, it is made clear that the
petitioner shall continue to pay maintenance in favour other respondents in
accordance with the order passed by the court below.
List on 29.07.2022 along with CRM (M) No. 463/2021.
Xerox copy of the trial court record be summoned.
