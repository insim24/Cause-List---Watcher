CrlM No. 1582/2023
1. Perusal of the record of the proceedings would reveal that in terms of
order dated 11.05.2023, Registrar Judicial (Jammu Wing of this
Court) came to be directed to hold an inquiry as in what
circumstances the Registry had issued a docket for release of the
appellant pursuant to order dated 30.03.2017 passed by this Court
without obtaining bail bonds and surety bonds from the appellant in
terms of the said order.
2. Perusal of the record tends to show that the Incharge Registrar
Judicial has preferred the instant application, seeking therein
extension of time for completion of the inquiry stating therein that
though the inquiry has been initiated in the matter, yet same is at its
preliminary stage and on account of retirement of the erstwhile

Registrar Judicial recently, the inquiry needs some more time to be
completed/concluded.
3. For the reasons stated in the application, same is allowed and one
month’s time as sought is granted for completion of the inquiry.
Application stands disposed of.
4. In the meantime, Registry is directed to secure the presence of the
appellant through issuance of non-bailable warrants at his Srinagar
address to be executed through SSP, Srinagar, without any fail.
