Heard on CM No. 6287/2023.
This is an application for condoning the delay of 26 days which has
occasioned in filing the restoration application. Pursuant to the order dated
07.08.2023 which had dismissed the appeal on account of non-appearance of
the counsel.
In view of what has been stated in the application, sufficient reasons
have been shown for condoning the delay. The application is therefore
allowed and the delay of 26 days in filing the restoration application is
condoned.
CM No. 6287/2023 is allowed.
CM No. 7005/2022
We have gone through the order dated 07.08.2023 which has
dismissed the appeal on the ground of non-appearance of learned counsel for
the petitioner and we deem it fit to restore the appeal to its original number
subject to payments of costs of Rs. 500/- to be deposited in the Litigants
Welfare Fund within two weeks time.
CM No. 7005/2022 is allowed.
List the main appeal on 11th December 2023.
