ORDER
28.03.2022
ASI Balraj, of Police Station, Kulgam, is present today before the Court
along with the Case Diary.
The dispute which has led to filing of the impugned FIR is between the
family members. The accused happen to be the wife and sons whereas the
complainant happens to be the father/husband.
The IO, who is present before the Court, has submitted that the parties
are at logger heads with each other and many cases have been filed by them
against each other. A perusal of the Case Diary which has been produced in
Court shows that efforts are on to settle the matter amicably.
Having regard to the nature of relationship between the complainant
and the petitioners, it would be better if the matter is settled amicably. In this
regard the IO shall make an endeavor to negotiate settlement between the
parties. He shall report to this court about the progress of settlement on next
date of hearing.
List on 17.05.2022.
Interim orders, if any, shall continue till next date of hearing.
