1. Upon coming of the instant matter for consideration, it
came to be noticed that the contesting parties are closely
related to each other being father, mother and sons, over a
piece of land situated at Laundana Udhampur, therefore,
it is deemed appropriate to explore a possibility of

amicable settlement inter se the parties and resolving of
their inter se disputes.
2. Accordingly, Registrar Judicial, Srinagar is directed to
refer the matter for mediation to be undertaken by Mr.
Nazir Ahmad Beigh, Senior Advocate present in the Court,
who has shown his willingness to mediate between the
parties.
3. The appearing counsel for the parties are directed to
ensure the presence of their respective parties before the
Mediator on the next date.
