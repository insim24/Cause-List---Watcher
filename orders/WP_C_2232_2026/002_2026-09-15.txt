HON’BLE MR. JUSTICE MOHD YOUSUF WANI, JUDGE
ORDER
15.09.2026
1. Issue notice to the respondents in the main as well as in the interim
application returnable by the next date of hearing subject to taking
of steps for service within a week’s period for filing of
reply/objections, if any.
2. Heard the learned counsel for the applicant/petitioner in respect of
his prayer for grant of relief in the interim application bearing No.
CM 6066/2026 and considered his submissions.
3. Perused the interim application supported with an affidavit. Also
perused the main petition and the copies of documents enclosed
with the same as annexures thereto.
4. It was inter alia submitted by the learned counsel for the
applicant/petitioner while praying for grant of interim relief that the
petitioner has been falsely and frivolously implicated in the case
FIR along with so many other persons for cultivation of Poppy

Crop. He submitted that the petitioner is totally innocent and has a
right to prove his innocence at the trial of the case, if any, put for
judicial adjudication.
5. He further contended that an accused is presumed to be innocent till
proved guilty at the conclusion of the trial.
6. He submitted that the applicant/petitioner is a registered
Government Contractor holding Registration Card No.
31/SE/Hyd/Hand/DEE/2012-13 and has been engaged in execution
of various government works. That he has already completed some
of the works and is also in the process of the execution of some
duly allotted works including the work at Parnew, Budgam and
other works under Sub-division Khansahib R&B. That the
impugned communication issued by the respondent No. 5 under No.
SC/BUD/4774-79, dated: 14.08.2026 is grossly in violation of his
fundamental rights guaranteed under Part-III of the Constitution of
India as also in contravention of the principles of natural justice. He
submitted that the petitioner has been punished before the
establishment of his guilt under law which is highly illegal.
7. Prima facie, case is made out for indulgence of the Court.
8. List on 23rd October, 2026.
9. In the meantime, the operation of the impugned communication
bearing No. SC/BUD/4774-79, dated: 14th August, 2026 issued by
the respondent No. 5 to the respondents Nos. 6 to 9 shall remain
stayed with the further direction to the non-applicants/respondents

to process the pending bills, if any, of the applicant/petitioner in
respect of the works already executed by him for payment against
the same, under rules.
(MOHD YOUSUF WANI)
