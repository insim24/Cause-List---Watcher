HON’BLE MR. JUSTICE MOHD YOUSUF WANI, JUDGE
ORDER
29.09.2026
1. Through the medium of the instant applications the applicant/petitioner
respectively seeks the impleadment of Executive Engineer Public Works
(R & B) Division, Budgam as respondent No.10 in the main writ petition
and the extension of the interim order already granted on 15.09.2026 to
his extent also, on the main ground that the impleadment of the said
person is necessary in the facts and circumstances of the case for the just
decision of the writ petition.
2. Heard the submissions of the learned counsel for the
applicant/petitioner in respect of the applications.
3. Perused the applications which are supported by dully attested
affidavits. On the grounds mentioned in the applications coupled with the
submissions made at the bar same are allowed and the Executive
Engineer Public Works (R & B)is impleaded as party respondent in the

main petition. The ad interim order dated 15.09.2026 shall be applicable
to his extent also.
4. The Registry is directed to make necessary entry in the cause title of the
petition. The learned counsel for the petitioner shall submit an amended
cause title.
5. Notice shall be issued to the newly arrayed respondent No.10,
returnable by the next date of hearing subject to taking of steps for service
within a week’s time for filing of his reply/objections.
6. The main matter shall come up at its schedule date, i.e., on 23rd
October 2026.
7. These applications are disposed of.
