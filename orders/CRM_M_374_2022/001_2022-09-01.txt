ORDER

1. The petitioners have challenged FIR No. 77/2022 for offences
under Sections 447, 354, 506, 427 IPC registered with Police
Station, Chariresharief, Budgam.
2. It has been contended by learned counsel for the petitioners that in
an earlier complaint relating to incident dated 27.04.2022 filed by
the private respondents , the police had submitted a report before the
concerned Magistrate indicating therein that no such occurrence had
taken place and instead it is the complainant party who had assaulted
the petitioners on the said date, regarding which the FIR No.
32/2022 for offences under Sections 341, 147, 148, 323, 307 IPC
stands registered and the investigation is going on.
3. Learned counsel has submitted that the learned Magistrate had
without taking into account the aforesaid report of the police

directed the registration of the impugned FIR when a second
application was made by the private respondents before him. It has
been contended that there is a civil dispute going on between the
petitioners and respondent No. 3 and 4 and that the said respondents,
have, in order to wreak vengeance upon the petitioners as a counter
blast to the FIR No. 32/2022 lodged the impugned FIR which is
absolutely false.
4. Having heard learned counsel for the petitioners and having perused
the material on record, a case for grant of interim indulgence is
made out.
5. Issue notice to the respondents. Notice be sent to the respondent No.
1 and 2 through Mr. Faheem Nisar, learned GA. Learned counsel for
the petitioners shall furnish copy of the petition to Mr. Faheem
Nisar, learned GA who may file his response by next date of
hearing. Notice be sent to respondent No. 3 and 4 through concerned
Police Station.
6. In the meantime, the investigation in the impugned FIR shall remain
stayed.
7. List on 07.10.2022.
