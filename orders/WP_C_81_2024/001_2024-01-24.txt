The petitioner has challenged a notice dated 28.12.2023 passed by
Assistant Executive Engineer, Electric Sub Division Chadoora, District
Budgam – respondent No. 04 whereby the petitioner has been fined Rs
33,29,388/- for alleged power meter tampering.
Learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the notice
has been issued without following the procedure.
Issue notice to the respondents subject to the petitioner taking
necessary steps for effecting service upon the respondents within a
week’s time.
List on 11.03.2024.
In the meanwhile, subject to the objections from the other side and
till next date of hearing, the impugned notice dated 28.12.2023 shall
remain stayed.
