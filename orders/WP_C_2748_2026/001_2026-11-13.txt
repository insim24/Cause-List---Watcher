ORDER
13.11.2026
1. This is an application seeking rectification of order dated 03.11.2026
passed by this Court whereby the writ petition was dismissed.
2. In the application, it has been submitted that the title of the writ petition
is wrongly mentioned inasmuch as the actual title of the petition is ‘Aasim
Facility Management and Securities Th. its Prop Vs. Union of India Th.
Secretary Civil Aviation and Others’ but in the order, the same has been
reflected as ‘Aasim Facility Management and Securities Th. Its Prop Vs.
Sajad Rasool Shah, Tehsildar Srinagar and another”. It appears to be a
typographical error and as such deserves to be corrected. Accordingly, it
is provided that in order dated 03.11.2026 passed in WP(C) No.
2748/2026 the title may be read as ‘Aasim Facility Management and
Securities Th. its Prop Vs. Union of India Th. Secretary Civil Aviation
and Others’. The aforesaid order stands modified to the above extent. This
order shall form part of the order dated 03.11.2026.
3. CM disposed of.
