Upon coming of the instant matter for consideration, it got revealed
that the petitioner herein is the son of the respondent/complainant who has filed a
complaint against the petitioner/son impugned in the instant petition.
Having regard to the nature of relationship between the parties
inasmuch as the issue involved in the instant petition, it is deemed appropriate to
explore a possibility of mediation/ amicable settlement inter se the parties.
Accordingly, learned counsel for the petitioner is directed to ensure
the presence of the petitioner either physically or through virtual mode on next date
of hearing.
Registry shall also issue a notice to the respondent herein through
SHO P/S Kulgam for ensuring his presence either physically or virtually.
List on 26th September 2025.
