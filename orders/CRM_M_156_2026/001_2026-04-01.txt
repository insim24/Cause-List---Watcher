CrlM 364/2026:
1. By this application, applicants herein are seeking permission of this Court
for dispensing with the requirement of filing certified copy of FIR No.
13/2026 of Police Station, Chanapora.
2. For the reasons stated in the application, same is allowed and the
requirement of filing certified copy of FIR No. 13/2026 is dispensed with
for the time being. Same shall be placed on record by or before next date
of hearing.
3. CrlM is disposed of.
CRM(M) 156/2026:
4. By this petition, petitioners herein have invoked jurisdiction of this Court
under Section 528 of BNSS, 2023, thereby, challenging FIR No. 13/2026,
dated 28.02.2026, registered at Police Station, Chanapora, under Sections
333 and 324(4) of BNSS, 2023.
5. It is stated by learned counsel for the petitioner that there a civil dispute
going on between the parties and out of vengeance, the impugned FIR has
been lodged.
6. Notice to the respondents, returnable within four weeks. Requisites within
one week.
7. List on 11.05.2026.
8. Meanwhile, subject to objections and till next date of hearing, the
concerned Investigating officer is directed to proceed ahead with the

investigation, however, challan shall not be presented before the
competent court without seeking prior permission of this Court.
