ORDER
14.08.2025
01. The respondent No. 1-Jandad Khan and
the respondent No. 2-Raja Khalid Iqbal joined
together as plaintiffs to file a civil suit on file
No. 24/N on 23.04.2021 before the Court of
learned Additional District Judge, Budgam
naming therein the respondent No. 3 herein-
Ghulam Qadir Hijam, the respondent No. 4-

Ghulam Hassan herein and one Abdul Raheem
S/o Sultan Hijam as three defendants.
02. The suit so filed by the respondents No. 1
and 2 was for simpliciter permanent
prohibitory injunction and claiming a decree to
the effect as set out in the plaint which is as
follow:
“a. A decree of permanent injunction be
passed in favour of the plaintiff and
against the defendants whereby the
defendants be permanently restrained
from causing or cause to be done any
sort of interference into the peaceful
possession/enjoyment of the plaintiffs
over the suit land measuring 32K.5M.
199/3, 216,212 691/218, 689/216,
697/214,313/216,215 situated at
Raiyar ich Tehsil Khansahib District
Budgam.
b. Any other relief/direction/order
which deems fit and proper in the

attending circumstances of the case
may also be passed in favour of the
plaintiffs and against the defendants.”
03. Said suit came to be decreed not by merit
based adjudication by the trial court of learned
Additional District Judge, Budgam by virtue of
a judgment dated 20.10.2021.
04. While relief sought in the suit was
originally for permanent prohibitory
injunction, the judgment and decree dated
20.10.2021 which came to be granted by the
trial court of learned Additional District Judge,
Budgam turned out to be that of declaration
literally acknowledging a state of partition with
respect to suit property amongst the parties to
the suit.
05. The passing of the judgment decree dated
20.10.2021 was made on the basis of a
purported compromise deed dated 20.10.2021
which is said to have been arrived at between
the parties to the suit and accordingly, the trial

court of learned Additional District Judge,
Budgam purportedly followed the mandate of
Order 23 of the Code of Civil Procedure, (CPC)
1908 and disposed of the suit as compromised.
06. The judgment and decree dated
20.10.2021 so passed by the Court of
Additional District Judge, Budgam always
counted to be a decree in personam i.e. inter se
the parties and not a decree in rem against the
rest of the world which includes the appellant
herein who feels aggrieved of the outcome of
the civil suit of the respondents No. 1 and 2
being decreed on a compromise basis
purportedly prejudicing the alleged claim and
possession of the appellant vis-à-vis the suit
property.
07. Be that as it may as the appellant herein
was a non-party in the suit so the present
appeal is to be maintained only upon getting
grant of leave to challenge the judgment and
decree.

08. The limitation period is coming in the way
of the appellant/applicant related to the filing
of civil first appeal under section 96 of the
Code of Civil Procedure, (CPC) 1908 for which
there is a condonation of delay application.
09. The defendant No. 2-Ghulam Hassan in
the suit who happens to be respondent No. 4
in the present case and the proforma
respondents No. 8 to 10 herein are brother and
sisters of the appellant/applicant.
10. The appellant/applicant in the matter of
seeking leave to appeal is, thus, pressing a
situation that she along with proforma
respondents 8 to 10 along with defendant No.
2-Ghulam Hassan (respondent No. 4) herein
are the legal heirs of their father-Ghulam
Rasool Hajam and thus was and were
stakeholders in the suit property which was
not amenable to be compromised by whim and
fancy of the defendant No. 2-Ghulam Hassan
Hajam thereby prejudicing the vested rights of

inheritance of the appellant as well as her
sisters who are the respondents 8 to 10 herein
11. The decree is sought to be impugned by the
appellant/applicant as being an act of fraud
and collusion operating between the two
plaintiffs and the defendants and in the
process seriously prejudicing the vested rights
of the other stakeholders which the appellant
claims herself to be as one of them.
12. In the given context of the facts and
circumstances of the case, a case is made out
for considering the application filed grant of
leave to appeal in favour of the
applicant/appellant and, therefore, an
application being CM No. 5193/2025 is to be
considered first.
13. Since this Court is to first consider the
leave to appeal in favour of the
applicant/appellant and as the appeal is time
barred, CMP No. 5192/2025 whereby
condonation of delay is being sought would

come for consideration only upon leave being
granted first.
14. Notice in CM No. 5193/2025 read with
application being CM No. 5194/2025.
15. Service of the non-applicants to take place
through Tamilat Section of the Principal
District Judge, Budgam.
16. The Registrar Judicial, Srinagar to issue
summons for service of all the 14 non-
applicants to be carried out under mandate of
Order 5 of the Code of Civil Procedure, (CPC)
1908.
17. In the meantime, till next date of hearing
operation of the impugned decree and
judgment dated 20.10.2021 passed by the
Court of learned Additional District Judge,
Budgam in civil suit on file No. 24/N of 2021
titled Jandad Khan and Anr Vs Ghulam
Qadir Hajam and Ors shall remain stayed and
further status qua obtaining with respect to
possession of suit property be maintained by

the applicants as well as by the non-
applicants.
18. List on 26th of September, 2025.
