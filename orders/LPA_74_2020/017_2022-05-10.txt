This appeal is against a composite judgment dated 17.03.2020 passed
by learned Single Judge which has given rise to LPA No. 74/2020.
In the aforesaid LPA, we have passed an order directing counsel for
the Jammu and Kashmir Public Service Commission and the General
Administration Department to file response to the application CM No.
2281/2022 seeking CBI enquiry in the matter.
A similar application CM No. 2329/2022 has been moved in this
appeal also.
Accordingly, we direct learned counsel for the Jammu and Kashmir
Public Service Commission and General Administration Department to file
response to the application in this case also within a period of two weeks.
List on 31.05.2022 along with LPA No. 74/2020.
