ORDER
The appellant, who appeared in person, inter-alia submitted that
as per the criteria fixed by the Commission minimum marks required in
English paper were 40%, whereas in other subjects for open merit candidates
the minimum marks required were 30% and for reserved category candidates
the minimum percentage was fixed 25% (page no. 246). Still, the candidates
who had secured marks less than 30% and were in open merit category were
shortlisted and called for interview (page no. 354). Most of them were
related with the members of the J&K Public Service Commission or senior
officers in the then State of J&K.
He further submitted that one of the candidate namely Sarib
Sehran s/o Yehdullah Dar, whose parents were senior level officers serving
in the then State at that time, was appointed one year after the initial

appointments were made showing that on re-checking of answer sheets, he
got 135 marks in one of the papers though initially he got only 35 marks. It
is despite the fact that initially when number of candidates had applied for
re-checking, the commission had responded by saying that there was no
change in the marks secured by any of the candidates and further this
candidate had not even applied for rechecking within the time permitted.
Notice to respondent Nos. 1 and 2 only at this stage, for
17.07.2020.
Mr. Azhar-Ul-Amin, Advocate accepts notice on behalf of
respondent No. 1 and Mr. Sajjad Ashraf, Government Advocate accepts
notice on behalf of respondent No. 2, on the asking of the Court.
On the next date of hearing, J&K Public Service Commission is
directed to produce entire record of the selection in question in Court in a
sealed cover. Relevant record pertaining to the minimum marks required in
written exam and the marks secured by the candidates as noticed above, who
were called for interview though did not secure minimum marks and the
answer sheets of Sarib Sehran s/o Yehdullah Dar in the paper where on
rechecking marks were increased, shall be clearly flagged.
In case of failure, the Secretary of the Commission shall
appear in court through VC. Learned Counsel appearing for the Commission
to coordinate.
