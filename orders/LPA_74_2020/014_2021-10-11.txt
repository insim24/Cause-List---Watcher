The application bearing CM No. 6657/2021, is seeking
advancement of date fixed in appeal bearing LPA No. 74/2020 C/w LPA
No. 82/2020 and LPA No. 117/2020.

for hearing on 18.10.2021, which has been declared as non-sitting day,
therefore, he prays for listing the matter on some other working day.

allowed and appeal bearing LPA No. 74/2020 C/w LPA No. 82/2020 and
LPA No. 117/2020 is re-fixed on 08.11.2021 as case no.1.
CM No. 6657/2021 is disposed of.
Copy of this order be placed on the record of appeal.
