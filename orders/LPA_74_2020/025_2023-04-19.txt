ORDER
19.04.2023
It is submitted by the learned counsel for the appellants that
this Court vide order dated 18.05.2021 directed the Registry to list the
matter on 06.07.2021 for final decision. Further, vide order dated
15.02.2023 the matter was directed to be listed for final
consideration/disposal with the liberty to make a mention.
It is further averred that despite directions by this Court to list
the matter for final consideration, the Registry is not listing the
matter on final hearing day. Though the matter was further listed on
09.05.2023 which is a hearing day.
Be that as it may, Registry is directed to list the matter on
09.05.2023 in final hearing column in terms of orders referred above.
Application stands disposed of.
