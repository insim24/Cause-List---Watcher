ORDER
05.06.2023
It has brought to the notice of this Court by Mr. T.M. Shamsi, learned
DSGI, that a petition involving the same issue is pending adjudication
before this Court bearing WP(C) No. 2248/2022 titled as Mrs. Grace Pal
Jor V.s Union of Territory of J&K & ors and seeks listing of the said writ
petition along with the present petition.
Mr. T.M.Shamsi, learned DSGI, is directed to place on record the
policy, if any, framed by the Ministry of Defence in the light of stand taken
by the respondents in para 7 of the reply, whereby the respondents have
stated that in a meeting dated 15.04.1991 in the Ministry of Defence as

conveyed vide MoD no. 11014/2 dated 10.05.1991, the status quo will be
maintained and will go for resumption of all the sites when the situation
normalizes in the erstwhile State. The respondents have also taken a
specific stand in the reply that they have decided that the leases will not be
further renewed which have expired or due to expire in the near future.
Let the respondents place on record the policy framed in the light of
the stand so taken by or before next date of hearing.
Registry is directed to club this petition along with the aforesaid
petition bearing no. WP(C) No. 2248/2022 and list on the date fixed in the
aforesaid petition.
(WASIM SADIQ NARGAL)
