ORDER
03.11.2023
Through this writ petition, the petitioners are posing a challenge,
by reference to Article 14 of the Constitution of India, in the context of their
exclusion from the benefit of Government Order no. 12-F of 2017 dated 12th
January 2017 in terms whereof flood affected Kisan Credit Card Holders
were meant to be relieved of the loan burden subject to the terms and
conditions as envisaged in Clause 1 (a to g).
As per the petitioners, the Government Order supra has excluded
the Kisan Credit Card Holders whose loan outstanding balance as on 31st
March 2015 was not below Rs. 1.00 lacs whereas they were equally hit by
natural calamity. The waiver which was extended by the Government in
favour of the eligible kisan credit card holders was to the extent of 50% of
the loan outstanding as on 31st March 2015.
For the purpose of carrying out implementation of the said
Government Order, an amount of Rs. 50.00 crores was released in favour of
the Agriculture Production Department through J&K Bank and the
settlement of the cases was to be carried out by J&K Bank.

In furtherance to the aforesaid Government Order granting Rs.
50.00 crores of financial assistance, another Government Order no. 58-F of
2017 dated 9th March 2017 came into picture thereby providing an additional
fund of Rs. 91,18,61,000/- for the same very purpose. In this Government
Order also, the petitioners felt themselves reckoned out of the waiver
assistance.
The petitioners are alleging that the classification carried out for
the benefit of above said two Government Orders is arbitrary and artificial
and without any rational and the exclusion of the petitioners and similarly
situated kisan credit card holder farmers whose loan accounts on the
specified date of 31st August 2014 were standard one.
Before proceeding further in the course of arguments, this Court
deems it essential to know from the respondents that out of total financial
assistance granted by the Government in terms of above said two
Government Orders how much actually came to be utilized and to how many
number of farmers as this data and statistics is required to be provided to
appreciate the challenge being posed by the petitioners that the exclusion of
flood hit farmers whose bank loan account was not obtaining less then
Rs. 1.00 lacs were kept away from getting the benefit of waiver to the extent
of 50% of their outstanding liability.
Let the needful input be provided by the respondents within a
period of four weeks.
List in continuation on 20th December 2023.
(Rahul Bharti)
