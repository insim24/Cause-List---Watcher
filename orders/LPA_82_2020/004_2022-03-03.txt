Learned counsel for the applicants submits that the case of the
applicants/appellants in LPA No. 82/2020 is entirely different vis-à-vis LPA
Nos. 74/2020 and 117/2020. He further submits that the case of the
appellants in the instant appeal is required to be heard separately.
Be that as it may, the appellants/applicants are well within their rights
to project their case notwithstanding the fact that both the other two LPAs
are taken up together along-with the instant appeal. As such, learned counsel
for the appellants Mr. Shafqat Nazir, does not press the present application.
The same is disposed of as such.
