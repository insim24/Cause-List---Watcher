In this appeal, an application CM No. 2281/2022 has been filed by the
appellants seeking direction for reference of the matter to the CBI for
investigation and for taking appropriate legal action against the then
Chairman, Members, Controller of Examination, Secretary to Jammu and

precisely for the reason that the record produced by Jammu and Kashmir
Public Service Commission in a matter before the Jammu wing speaks
differently than the record or the affidavit which has been produced/ filed,
here in Srinagar.

Kashmir Public Service Commission and Sh. Sajad Ashraf, learned GA
appearing for the respondent No. 2-General Administration Department are
directed to file reply to the above application within two weeks’.

31.05.2022.
