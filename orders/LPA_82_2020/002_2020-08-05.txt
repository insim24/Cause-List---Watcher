ORDER
Mr. Azhar Ul Amin, learned counsel appearing for the
respondent No.1-J&K Public Service Commission submitted that the
relevant record of the commission has been sent to the Registrar Judicial of
Jammu Bench of this Court. He further submitted that even the record which
was lying with the Registry of this Court at Srinagar has also been sent to
Jammu. The Registrar Judicial, Jammu Bench confirmed receipt of record.

2 LPA No. 74/2020
Mr. Sajad Ashraf, learned counsel appearing for the respondent
No.2 submitted that intimation has been sent to the other private respondents
about the pendency of the present appeal, however, affidavit could not be
filed on account of restrictions imposed at Srinagar. He has placed before us
the letter which has been sent to private respondents.
Let the affidavit be filed on or before the next date of hearing.
A request has been made by the learned counsel for the
Commission and the private respondents that hard copies of the paperbook
will be provided to them as they are not able to attend their office on account
of restrictions imposed at Srinagar.
The appellants undertake to do the needful before the next date
of hearing.
