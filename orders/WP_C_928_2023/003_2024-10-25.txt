ORDER
25-10-2024
1. The instant application has been filed by the petitioner for
interim relief.
2. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that during pendency
of the instant writ petition, the private respondent has encroached
upon land of the petitioner bearing survey no. 202, village Chek
Bindoo Kokernag and have also stolen away the crop of walnut trees.
It is further submitted that the petitioner being aggrieved of the action
of the private respondent filed two applications, one before the
Tehsildar Kokernag on 18th September, 2024 and another before the
Station House Officer, Chek Bindoo Kokernag on the same date,
however, the concerned authorities have not taken any action
against the said respondent.
3. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner.

4. Issue notice, which is accepted by Mr. Jehagnir Ahmad Dar,
learned Government Advocate on behalf of the official respondents.
Learned GA shall file action taken report within two weeks’
positively.
5. List this application along with main case on already fixed date
in the main matter i.e. 18th November, 2024.
