ORDER
27.11.2026
01. The Petitioners have thrown challenge to FIR No. 80/2026
dated 1st of September, 2026 registered at Police Station Zakura, for the
commission of offences under Sections 326 (a), 324 (2) and 3 (5) of the
Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 [BNS], allegedly arising out of illegal
encroachment upon the Government/ irrigation land and filling/
obstruction of irrigation canals falling under Survey No. 454 Mouza Gasu,
Hazratbal.
02. The impugned FIR appears to have been lodged on the basis of
a complaint alleging indiscriminate dumping and erection of earth which
has caused considerable loss to the drainage and irrigation system of the
area.
03. It is in the aforesaid backdrop that the impugned FIR has been
lodged against the Petitioners, who are alleged to have indulged in
dumping of the garbage over the said area, including the irrigation canal,
which is stated to have been choked.
04. The principal argument of the learned Counsel for the
Petitioners is that there is neither any irrigation canal nor has any garbage/
waste material been dumped over the area. In support of this assertion, the

Petitioners have relied upon a reply received by them through Right to
Information Act (RTI) Act, furnished by the I&FC Department.
05. Although, the Petitioners have sought to substantiate their
defence that they neither indulged in any activity having deleterious effects
on the environment nor did their acts of omission or commission result in
the choking of the canal, however, keeping in view the wider
consequences of the alleged act, it is not advisable to stifle the
investigation at this stage. The offences alleged are cognizable and it is the
statutory duty of the Investigating Officer to carry out the investigation
without any obstruction. The investigation appears to be at its inception
and, in case the requisite material is collected supporting the allegations, it
will not only have consequences for the life and liberty of the complainant,
but will also carry wider health hazards and public ramifications.
06. In view of the above, no case for interference is made out at
this stage. Accordingly, the Petition is dismissed in limine, along with the
connected CrlM.
